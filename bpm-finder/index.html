How to find the BPM of a song

Start the track, listen for a steady pulse and tap once for each beat. After the second tap, the calculator converts the average interval between taps into beats per minute. Keep tapping for several bars to make the estimate more stable.

What BPM means

BPM stands for beats per minute. A tempo of 120 BPM means two beats occur each second. Producers, DJs, musicians and dancers use BPM to compare tempo, synchronize material and plan transitions.

BPM finder FAQ

How many taps should I use?

Four to eight steady taps is usually enough for a useful estimate. More taps can reduce the effect of one early or late tap.

Why does the result reset?

A pause longer than two seconds is treated as the start of a new tempo measurement.
