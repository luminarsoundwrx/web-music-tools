How to use the virtual piano

Click or tap a key, or use the mapped computer keyboard keys shown on the piano. Choose a triad mode to turn each played note into a major, minor or diminished chord. Add a major or minor seventh with the seventh controls.

The audio engine and piano samples are deferred until you interact with the tool, which avoids downloading the full piano library for visitors who never play a note.

Keyboard shortcuts

Use 1 and 2 to change octave. Use Z for single notes, M for major triads, N for minor triads and , for diminished triads. X, B and V control seventh extensions.

Virtual piano FAQ

Does this work with a MIDI keyboard?

Yes, in browsers that support the Web MIDI API. Permission is requested after the audio engine starts.

Why does audio take a moment the first time?

The page intentionally postpones Tone.js and the sampled piano files until they are needed. This keeps the initial page lighter.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The piano remains horizontally scrollable on narrow screens so the keys keep a usable size.
