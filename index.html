What the harmonizer generates

The tool starts with a four-note close voicing under the selected melody note. Drop techniques then move selected inner voices down by an octave to open the spacing. The MIDI export preserves the generated notes for use in a DAW.

Deferred audio and notation

VexFlow is loaded only when you generate notation, while Tone.js and piano samples load only when you press Play. That keeps the first page load lighter for visitors who only need the note names or MIDI export.

Voice harmonizer FAQ

What is Drop 2?

Drop 2 takes the second-highest voice of a close-position four-note chord and moves it down one octave, creating a more open voicing.

What does the approach option do?

It creates a parallel approach voicing by moving the generated target voices by the same interval as the approach melody note.
